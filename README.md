# Electric Vehicle (EV) Market & Population Analysis (Tableau Dashboard)

An interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing electric vehicle adoption trends, geographic distribution, manufacturer breakdown, and Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) eligibility.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview
The dashboard provides a comprehensive look into EV registration and market penetration, featuring key performance indicators (KPIs) and dynamic filters for deep-dive exploration.

* **Total Vehicles Analyzed:** 150,413
* **Average Electric Range:** 67.83 Miles
* **Total BEV Vehicles:** 116,745 (77.62%)
* **Total PHEV Vehicles:** 33,668 (22.38%)

## 🔍 Key Dashboard Components & Visuals

1. **KPI Scorecards:** High-level metrics tracking total vehicles, average electric range, and splits between Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV).
2. **Global Filters:** Interactive sidebar filters allowing users to slice data by **Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) Eligibility**, **EV Type**, **Model**, and **State**.
3. **Total Vehicles by Model Year (Trend Line):** Illustrates the rapid growth trajectory of EV registrations from 2012 through 2023, accompanied by average registration benchmarks.
4. **Total Vehicles by State (Geographic Map):** A Choropleth map highlighting state-by-state registration volumes across the United States.
5. **Top 10 Total Vehicles by Make (Bar Chart):** Ranks top manufacturers (led heavily by Tesla, Nissan, and Chevrolet) along with vehicle counts and percentage shares, driven by a dynamic `Top N` parameter.
6. **Total Vehicles by CAFV Eligibility (Donut Chart):** Breaks down the proportion of vehicles eligible, not eligible, or with unknown CAFV status.
7. **Total Vehicles by Model (Detailed Table):** Tabular view breaking down specific models, manufacturers, EV types, total volumes, and overall percentage shares.
